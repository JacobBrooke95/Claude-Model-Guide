Claude Capability Forecast 

What this is. A statistical extrapolation with stated priors. It estimates where the next model in each Claude family will land on Epoch's public Capabilities Index (ECI), on Anthropic's own index (AECI) as printed in that model's system card, and on several benchmarks, and it projects Anthropic's best model on the index to the end of 2030.

What it is not. It is not a roadmap and it uses no inside information. The near term (to ) is covered by a backtest of the projection code on past data, with the limits stated on the chart. Everything after that date is a scenario projection: a weighted mix of five stated scenarios whose weights are judgement, labelled as such on the chart.

Estimated scores for Anthropic's next models, and where the frontier may be by 2030.
