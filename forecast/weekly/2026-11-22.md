## What changed this week

**A second quiet week for releases, and a busy one for everything around them: nothing shipped, no sighting was logged, Anthropic went public, and the Opus forecast and the market moved apart.** By Dec 31 the model gives the next Opus 58% and Polymarket 84%. The record, such as it is, favours the market.

**What shipped.** Nothing since Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on Nov 9. The release notes, news page, models overview, pricing page and support notes were fetched on each of the seven days and show no new model (the only release-notes change was a Nov 16 entry on text watermarking for older models). No dated question resolved, so the scorecard is unchanged: model Brier 0.026 over 4 release cycles; against the market 0.077 to 0.005 over the 2 cycles where both had a number. Two cycles is an anecdote, and it is one the model is losing. (The "59 questions resolved" line above is a row count.)

- The model drifted down all week with no input behind it: a day off each checkpoint every day, the cadence refit, and one grade-C routing report from Oct 11 ageing. That is all it knows about Opus.
- The market fell hard early in the week (86% to 69% by Dec 31 in two days, on real volume, cause not found) and then came back to 84% while selling the Nov 30 rung down from 35% to 15% ($34,557 traded on that rung, $8,746 on December).
- On Nov 20 the two swapped sides on Nov 30: the model is now the higher one (17% against 15%).
- **What each is implying.** The market says: not Thanksgiving week, then very probably December. If no Opus ships by Nov 30, its prices put about 81% on a December release. The model, given the same condition, puts about 49% on December and leaves 42% for after New Year. Opus 5.5 is 61 days old; the last three Opus gaps were 42, 57 and 60 days, so this gap is already longer than each of those three, and a release after Dec 31 would be a gap of more than 100 days. The market reads that as "due"; the model's pooled prior still carries the slower 2024–25 rhythm and spreads the date out.
- **Which to believe.** On the one cycle where they differed (Fable 5.2), the market was higher and right. The 50/50 blend is 16% by Nov 30 and 73% by Dec 31, and where the model is below the market the blend or the market is the better single number. Where the model is above it (Nov 30, by 2 points) the difference is too small to mean anything.
