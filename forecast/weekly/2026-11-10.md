## What changed this week

**About this run.** It is off-cycle: made on Monday Nov 9, the day Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 shipped and one day after the regular Nov 8 run. The file is dated Nov 10 because that is the forecast origin. The engine counts only releases dated before its origin, so a Nov 9 origin would not have seen the release: it would have forecast a Fable that had already shipped, and `weekly.py` would then have graded that snapshot as a forecast made beforehand. A Nov 10 origin means "data through Nov 9". No Nov 9 snapshot or log rows were written.

**What shipped.** Claude Fable 5.2 (general availability) and Claude Mythos 5.2 (verified organizations only) on Nov 9, announced together at [anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2) and in the [platform release notes](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/release-notes/overview), with one [system card](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-5-2-mythos-5-2-system-card) for both. Price $8 / $40 per million tokens, down from $10 / $50, per the [pricing page](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing). All four pages were opened on Nov 9. Both rows are in the release table as co-launch group `c52`.

- The Nov 8 forecast gave Fable 6% by Nov 9 (Polymarket: 11.5%), 22% for Nov 8–12 and 45% by Nov 30 (market: 89%). Its median was Dec 4, 25 days late, and its 80% window opened on Nov 10, so the release fell one day outside it. Mythos: 8% by Nov 9, median Nov 24, also one day outside.

**Proposed changes.** None were made to the model. Five things for Jacob to decide:
1. *Release-day runs.* Either document "use the next day as the origin when a release shipped today" in the runbook, or have `weekly.py` refuse an origin equal to a release date already in the table. This run did the first by hand.
2. *The named-version weight.* 85% on an artifact's version string is a rule with one forward test, and it missed. Catalog rows and page headings can carry working names. A lower weight, or one that needs the name on a first-party API identifier, would be more defensible. `named_versions.json` is now empty for Fable and Mythos.
3. *Market parsing.* `engine/market.py` still matches "Next Fable Model (5.2+) released by". It is harmless while no such rows are in the market file, but the regex needs the new wording once Polymarket lists a ladder for the next Fable. I did not guess the name.
4. *Aged sightings.* The model discounted the October sightings each week they went unfulfilled, and the release came anyway. One case is not grounds to refit, but it is the second place (after the 2025–26 holdout) where the model was too slow, and it is worth testing a slower decay on the backtest before the next Fable-class cycle.
5. *Data kept in `engine/`.* `engine/params.py` holds the SWE-bench Pro table that mirrors the guide's chart. I added Fable 5.2's 93.0 there so the next projection starts from the current score. That is a data row, not a model change, but it lives in a directory the runbook says to leave alone, so it may belong in `data/`.
